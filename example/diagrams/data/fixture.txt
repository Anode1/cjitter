# cycle: the unit square without its diagonals. By symmetry a square is the stress-optimal
# shape of a 4-cycle, so at the fitted scale it is a stress minimum: pair ratios r/d are 1
# (four edges) and sqrt2/2 (two diagonals at two hops), L = (4 + 1/2) / (4 + sqrt2) = 0.923495,
# stress = (4 (1/L - 1)^2 + 2 (sqrt2/2/L - 1)^2) / 6 = 0.022876. Under the median L = 1 the
# diagonals are too short and every corner has an outward move that lowers the stress.
